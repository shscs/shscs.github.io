01 / About

A place to build, break, and learn.

The Computer Science Club is open to every student at Sutherland High School, whether you have written thousands of lines of code or none at all. We learn from each other, work on projects, and have fun along the way.

  • Beginner friendly
  • Learn by building
  • Open to every student

02 / Activities

What we explore.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions that take you from your first program to something you are proud to show off.

Projects

Team up to design and ship websites, apps, and games from idea to launch.

Challenges

Sharpen your problem-solving with puzzles and friendly coding competitions.

Web development

Learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then put your own work on the internet.

Security

Understand how systems get attacked, and how to build things that hold up.

Git & open source

Pick up the tools real developers use and learn to collaborate on shared code.
