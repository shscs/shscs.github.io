Meeting info ↓

What we do

  • Workshops

    Someone shows how a thing works, then everyone tries it. No hour-long slideshows.

  • Projects

    Websites, games, bots, tools. Small teams, real code, an actual repo.

  • Puzzles

    Algorithm problems and the occasional contest, for people who enjoy that sort of thing.

  • Security

    How things get broken and how to stop it, capture-the-flag style.

  • Git

    Branches, pull requests, and fixing the merge conflict you definitely caused.

Join

You don't need to know anything yet. If you're curious how software works, that's enough.
